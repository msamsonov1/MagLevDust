1. Maxwell Stress Tensor & Levitation Equilibrium

The primary upward suspension force $F_{\text{mag}}$ counteracting rover payload mass $m_L$ and chassis dynamic terrain reaction forces $F_{\text{dyn}}$ is generated by modulating the electromagnetic pole flux density $B(t)$ across physical airgap $z_g$:

$$F_{\text{mag}}(z_g, I) = \frac{B^2(z_g, I) \cdot A_{\text{pole}}}{2\mu_0} = \frac{\mu_0 N^2 I^2 A_{\text{pole}}}{4 z_g^2}$$

Dynamic equilibrium of the suspension bogie is governed by Newton's second law along the vertical axis, with lunar gravity $g_{\text{moon}} \approx 1.62\,\text{m/s}^2$:

$$m_{\text{eff}} \ddot{z}_g = F_{\text{mag}}(z_g, I) - F_{\text{MR}}(\dot{z}_g, B) - \left[ m_{\text{eff}} g_{\text{moon}} + F_{\text{terrain}}(t) \right]$$

2. Soft Magnetic Saturation & Viscoplastic MR Clamping

When the rover wheel descends off a sharp basalt boulder or crater rim ledge, the required force $F$ surges past $12\,\text{kN}$. High-saturation Fe-Co Permendur alloy cores saturate at $B_{\text{sat}} \approx 1.85\,\text{T}$, causing non-linear loss of levitation stiffness $\partial F / \partial z \to 0$ and catastrophic chassis bottoming.

$$B(H) = B_{\text{sat}} \tanh\left(\frac{\mu_r \mu_0 H}{B_{\text{sat}}}\right), \quad \text{where } H = \frac{N I}{2 z_g}$$

MagLevDust overcomes this ceiling by dumping energy into a secondary hermetic Bingham viscoplastic Magneto-Rheological (MR) shear layer without mechanical contact:

$$\tau_{\text{yield}} = \tau_0 + \alpha_{\text{MR}} B^2 \implies F_{\text{MR}} = \frac{A_{\text{shear}} L}{d} \left[ \tau_{\text{yield}} \operatorname{sgn}(\dot{z}_g) + \eta_0 \dot{z}_g \right]$$
Yield Multiplier $\alpha_{\text{MR}} = 45\,\text{kPa/T}^2$ | Dynamic MR Engagement: $\Delta t < 2.5\,\text{ms}$

3. Phase-Space Observer & Saturation Proximity Metric

The on-board Radiation-Hardened DSP runs an extended Kalman state observer at $10\,\text{kHz}$, estimating instantaneous force load $\hat{F}_k$ and coil magnetic flux $\hat{B}_k$. The software calculates an instantaneous proximity index $\Gamma_{\text{sat}}$:

$$\Gamma_{\text{sat}}(t) = \frac{B(t)}{B_{\text{sat}}} + \lambda_{\text{dyn}} \left( \frac{\dot{F}_{\text{load}}}{F_{\text{max}}} \right) \ge \Gamma_{\text{threshold}} \approx 0.92$$

When $\Gamma_{\text{sat}} \ge 0.92$, the controller freezes further electromagnetic coil current escalation (preventing thermal thermal-runaway in vacuum) and activates high-voltage solid-state gating to energize the secondary MR coil, clamping vertical velocity $\dot{z}_g \to 0$ smoothly.

$$I_{\text{MR}}(t) = K_d \cdot \left[ \frac{\dot{z}_g}{\max(z_g - z_{\text{min}}, \epsilon)} \right] \cdot \Theta(\Gamma_{\text{sat}} - 0.92)$$
